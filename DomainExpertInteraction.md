## Core Workflows

### Workflow 1: Asset Request and Allocation
- **Trigger:** User submits an asset request  
- **Steps:**
  1. User raises request through the system  
  2. Office Admin reviews the request  
  3. Request is escalated if required  
  4. Approval or rejection is recorded  
  5. Staff allocates the asset  
- **Outcome:** Asset allocated or request rejected  

---

### Workflow 2: Asset Maintenance
- **Trigger:** User reports an issue with an asset  
- **Steps:**
  1. User submits issue report  
  2. Staff reviews the issue  
  3. Maintenance activity is performed  
  4. Maintenance history is updated  
- **Outcome:** Asset restored and issue closed  

---

### Workflow 3: New Asset Entry
- **Trigger:** Procurement of a new asset  
- **Steps:**
  1. Staff enters asset details into the system  
  2. Asset is assigned to an office  
  3. Asset becomes available for allocation  
- **Outcome:** Asset available for requests  

---

## Rules, Constraints, and Exceptions

- **Mandatory Rules:**
  - Assets must be approved before allocation  
  - Maintenance records must be maintained  

- **Constraints:**
  - Limited asset availability  
  - Approval hierarchy must be followed  

- **Common Exceptions:**
  - Emergency allocations may bypass standard escalation  

- **Failure Scenarios:**
  - Delayed approvals  
  - Missing or incorrect asset records
